**ZENKAI Power!**
---
**Version: 1.1.0**

**Added**

- *Sparking! Changes*:
  - Manual lock-on control: hold L1 to unlock from the opponent. Hold L1 again to start searching.
  - Inspired by Sparking! NEO / Budokai Tenkaichi 2, Goku (Mid)'s Spirit Bomb now appears above the stage after gathering energy and grows with each charge.
  - On Android, drag an empty area on the right half of the screen to rotate the camera horizontally while unlocked from the opponent.
- Android mobile version:
  - Drag across the screen to orbit the camera in *Character Reference*'s Model View.
  - During *Sparking! Changes* battles, 
  - R1 + Triangle combination button for the virtual gamepad.
- Optional **Show FPS** setting under *Video Settings*, displaying the frame rate during battles.

**Changed**

- Renamed *Frame Rate (FPS)* video setting to *Frame Interpolation*.

**Fixed**

- *Classic Mode* now retains the original training behavior, without team battle and the extra *Training Settings*.
- Frame Interpolation:
  - Interpolation geometry matching and texture-atlas handling, reducing mismatched surfaces and animated-texture artifacts.
  - Character models flattening or distorting during interpolated turns, and unsafe stage-camera blends during large camera changes.
  - Interpolation frame pacing under heavy load, including delayed overload protection, underestimated render times, and overdue intermediate frames.
  - Pending real frames being replaced by new drawing data or having their textures changed before presentation. Overloaded interpolation now falls back to real frames and restores intermediate frames gradually.
